# Privacy Policy for ClarioMark

**Last updated: September 30, 2026**

ClarioMark is a field inspection and photo reporting application developed and published by Quiet Grove.

This Privacy Policy explains how ClarioMark handles information when you use the application.

## 1. Information We Collect

ClarioMark does not require you to create an account and does not collect, transmit, or store your personal information on servers operated by Quiet Grove.

Project information, photos, annotations, findings, report information, company logos, inspector names, and other content created or imported by the user are stored locally on the user's device.

Quiet Grove does not have access to this content.

## 2. Photos and Camera

ClarioMark allows users to:

- Take photos using the device camera.
- Import existing images from the device.
- Add annotations, measurements, labels, and findings to images.
- Include images in generated reports.

Camera access is used only when the user chooses to capture a photo.

Photos selected or captured by the user are used only for the projects and reports created within the application. ClarioMark does not upload these photos to servers operated by Quiet Grove.

## 3. Files and Storage

ClarioMark may access files or media selected by the user in order to import photos, company logos, project backups, or other supported content.

The application may also create files requested by the user, including PDF reports and ClarioMark project backup files.

ClarioMark accesses only the files or locations necessary to perform actions initiated by the user.

## 4. Project Data and Reports

Information entered into ClarioMark, including project names, locations, inspector information, notes, findings, captions, and annotations, is stored locally on the user's device.

PDF reports are generated locally on the device.

When a user chooses to share or export a report, backup, image, or other file, the destination is selected by the user through Android's sharing or file selection features.

Quiet Grove does not receive copies of exported or shared files.

## 5. Backup and Import

ClarioMark allows users to export and import project backup files.

Backup files may contain project information, photos, annotations, findings, and other project-related content.

These files are created and stored at locations selected by the user. Quiet Grove does not automatically upload, receive, or store these backups.

Users are responsible for protecting backup files that contain confidential or sensitive project information.

## 6. In-App Purchases

ClarioMark may offer optional paid features through Google Play.

Purchases are processed by Google Play. Quiet Grove does not receive or store payment card numbers or other payment credentials.

Google may process information related to purchases according to Google's own privacy policies.

ClarioMark may receive information from Google Play necessary to determine whether a user is entitled to purchased features.

## 7. Internet Access

ClarioMark does not use an account-based cloud service operated by Quiet Grove and does not upload project content, photos, reports, or user-entered information to Quiet Grove servers.

Some services provided by the Android operating system or Google Play, including purchase verification and application distribution, may require an internet connection and are governed by the privacy policies of their respective providers.

## 8. Advertising

ClarioMark does not display third-party advertising.

## 9. Analytics and Tracking

ClarioMark does not use third-party advertising trackers.

Quiet Grove does not use ClarioMark to track users across applications or websites.

## 10. Data Sharing

Quiet Grove does not sell user data.

Quiet Grove does not share project content, photos, reports, annotations, findings, or other user-created content with third parties.

Content is shared with another application or service only when the user explicitly chooses to export or share it using Android's system features.

## 11. Data Retention and Deletion

Because ClarioMark stores project information locally, users control the retention of their project data.

Users may delete projects and associated content from within the application. Uninstalling ClarioMark will normally remove application data stored by the application, subject to Android device backup, storage, and file-management behavior.

Files that users have independently exported, shared, or backed up may remain in their selected storage locations or third-party services until deleted by the user.

## 12. Children's Privacy

ClarioMark is intended as a professional productivity tool and is not specifically directed toward children.

Quiet Grove does not knowingly collect personal information from children through ClarioMark.

## 13. Security

ClarioMark is designed to keep project information and user-created content locally on the user's device.

Users should protect their device and exported files appropriately, particularly when projects contain confidential, proprietary, or personally identifiable information.

## 14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when ClarioMark's functionality changes or when required by applicable laws, regulations, or platform requirements.

Changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
